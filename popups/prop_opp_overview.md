Information in this field should provide other team members with a general understanding of the property as well as context for the opportunity. This should include but is not limited to a description of the property, why it may be of interest, how it came to our attention, ecological information and strategic concerns. The goal should be to allow other team members to understand the opportunity briefly and discern it from other similar entries. While more information is generally better, there is value in being concise and not duplicating information captured in other fields.
