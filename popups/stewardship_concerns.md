This field is intended to document any known or potential issues, liabilities, or management considerations associated with a property that could affect its long-term stewardship, cost, risk, or feasibility. Entries should be precautionary in nature and capture concerns that warrant further investigation, monitoring, or mitigation, even if they are not yet fully confirmed. This section is not intended to disqualify a property, but to ensure that stewardship implications are clearly identified, communicated, and considered during securement decisions and long-term planning.
